Vision

I chose to pursue Industrial Design because I believe it brings together many areas of design and allows for a comprehensive, collaborative approach to problem-solving. Industrial Design offers the opportunity to work on diverse projects, enabling me to grow professionally across different fields while combining strategic thinking, aesthetic sensibility, and technology to create real and innovative solutions.

I see myself as a self-taught and curious person. Since I was a child, I've explored topics that caught my interest through hands-on learning. This has made me a persistent individual and helped me understand the value of process in achieving the best outcomes.

When I was 18 years old, I had the chance to visit the Cooper Hewitt Museum in New York, in which I was able to see a product that changed my whole design perspective: a shirt that could give people with hearing problems the experience of "feeling the music" through vibrations.

My professional patch suddenly became very clear: I wanted to change people's lives. I realized Industrial Design is not only about developing fancy products, it's about identifying overlooked spaces where action is needed.

View video

My motivation comes from moments when design becomes a bridge: between people and experiences, between limitations and possibilities. Seeing how a simple idea can improve someone's quality of life or shift their perspective is what fuels my curiosity and commitment.

As an Industrial Designer, I'm especially inspired by projects that combine creativity, empathy, and innovation. I'm motivated by the challenge of working across disciplines, exploring new formats, and designing with intention—always seeking to make a positive impact, no matter the scale.

Since childhood, I've been captivated by the world of design. It all started with a notebook filled with sketches of dresses and accessories, dreaming of becoming a fashion designer someday. As I grew up, I began to notice how my curiosity expanded—drawn to architecture while traveling, intrigued by furniture and interior spaces at friends' homes, and inspired by clever, everyday objects in local shops.

Over time, I realized that my passion wasn't limited to fashion—it was design itself, in all its expressions, that truly moved me.

After studying Industrial Design, my interests have evolved toward user experience and visual communication. I'm excited by the idea of blending different areas of design to create solutions that are holistic, thoughtful, and full of creativity.

Let's work together!

If you are interested in my work or have any questions, feel free to contact me. I am always open to new opportunities and collaborations.
